Pricing

What actually changes a moving quote

There's no fixed national rate for packers and movers — costs vary by vendor and city. Here's what tends to move the number up or down.

Because Verified Movers & Packers is a marketplace rather than a mover itself, we don't set prices — every quote comes from the vendor who'd actually do the job. But across the requests we route, a handful of factors consistently explain why two quotes for what looks like "the same move" can differ:

  • Distance and route — a local shift and an interstate move draw on completely different cost structures (fuel, tolls, driver time).
  • Inventory size — a 1BHK and a 4BHK need different truck sizes and crew counts, which is why "move size" is the first thing we ask for.
  • Timing — weekends, month-ends, and the April–June peak season typically see higher demand and less vendor flexibility on price.
  • Access — narrow lanes, no-lift buildings, and long carry distances from the parking spot to your door all add labor time.
  • Packing quality — premium materials (wooden crating, foam-lined boxes) cost more than standard cardboard and bubble wrap, but protect fragile or high-value items better.
  • Extra services — unpacking, furniture disassembly/reassembly, and short-term storage are usually quoted separately from the base move.

This is exactly why every Verified Movers & Packers quote breaks these out as separate line items instead of one bundled number — so you can see which factor is driving the price, and compare that same breakdown across every vendor who quotes you.

What's included

Packing materials vendors typically use

Every vendor sets their own materials and pricing — this is what to ask about when comparing quotes.

Packing quality is one of the biggest swing factors between a smooth move and a damaged-goods dispute, so it's worth knowing what "good packing" actually involves before you compare quotes:

For everyday items

Corrugated boxes in a few standard sizes, packing paper for wrapping crockery and glassware, and packing tape to seal everything shut are the baseline. Wardrobe boxes with a hanging bar save time on clothes that would otherwise need folding and re-hanging.

For fragile and high-value items

Bubble wrap and foam sheets cushion glassware, electronics, and artwork against shocks in transit. Custom wooden crates are typically used for oversized or particularly delicate pieces — mirrors, large artwork, aquariums.

For furniture

Furniture blankets protect wood and upholstery from scratches during loading and transit, and stretch/plastic wrap keeps drawers and doors secure so they don't swing open mid-move.

When you're comparing quotes, it's reasonable to ask each vendor exactly which of these materials are included at their quoted price, versus offered as a paid upgrade — that's the kind of detail a standardised, itemised quote should make clear upfront.

Before you book

Common mistakes people make hiring movers

Most bad moving experiences trace back to one of these — all avoidable with the right questions upfront.

01

Booking on a verbal quote

Get the price breakdown in writing before you pay any advance — verbal estimates are where "hidden charges" come from.

02

Not checking GST registration

An unregistered, unverifiable vendor has little accountability if something goes wrong. Every vendor on Verified Movers & Packers is GST-verified.

03

Skipping insurance

Transit insurance is inexpensive relative to the value it protects — confirm coverage before move day, not after.

04

Comparing bundled quotes

A single bundled number hides which line item is expensive. Ask for base fare, packing, labor, and insurance broken out separately.

05

Not supervising loading

Being present while your items are packed and loaded is the simplest way to catch handling issues before they become damage claims.

06

Booking too late

Weekends and month-ends fill up fast. Filing your request 4-5 days ahead gives you more vendors to actually compare.

Clear expectations

A better move starts before moving day.

Before confirming a booking, use this quick checklist with the moving provider so you know what the quote includes.

01

Get the price breakdown

Confirm transport, labour, packing materials, taxes and any extra charges in writing.

02

Confirm what is packed

Agree which items need special wrapping, who supplies materials and what is included in the quoted scope.

03

Discuss access and timing

Share floor, lift, stairs, parking and carry-distance details at pickup and delivery.

04

Ask about transit protection

Ask the provider whether insurance or transit protection is available, what it covers, and how claims are handled.
