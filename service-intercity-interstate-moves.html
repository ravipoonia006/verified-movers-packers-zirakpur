About intercity & interstate moves

Intercity and interstate shifting is for when pickup and drop are in different cities. Pricing usually reflects distance, truck type, packing level and whether the move is door-to-door. Share both cities and preferred dates on WhatsApp for a clearer discussion.

Popular intercity routes

Common requests include Zirakpur–Delhi NCR, Mohali–Jaipur, Chandigarh–Ludhiana, and Tricity to Mumbai, Bangalore or Hyderabad. Always share both city names and preferred delivery window.

What affects intercity cost

  • Distance and highway route
  • Truck type and whether the load is exclusive or shared
  • Packing level and fragile inventory
  • Loading/unloading floors at both ends

FAQ — intercity moves

How many days does transit take? It depends on distance and whether the truck is dedicated. Ask for an estimated delivery window on WhatsApp.

Is insurance included? Confirm transit insurance on every quote before you pay any advance.
