About · Zirakpur · Tricity

We help you move with clear quotes — not confusion.

Verified Movers & Packers is a quote desk based in Zirakpur. We prepare your move request and connect you on WhatsApp so you can talk directly about packing, transport and timing.

Who we are

Verified Movers & Packers operates from Zirakpur, Punjab, serving families and offices across the Chandigarh Tricity — Zirakpur, Mohali, Chandigarh and Panchkula, and nearby localities.

We are a marketplace-style quote desk, not a single truck fleet claiming to do every move ourselves. Your request is organised into a clear slip and opened on WhatsApp so you can discuss details with our team and matched vendors without endless broker phone tag.

What we do

  • Collect your pickup, drop, move size, date and contact details
  • Prepare a structured request for a faster WhatsApp conversation
  • Help you compare transparent, itemised expectations (labour, packing, transport)
  • Support local house shifting, office moves, intercity relocation and vehicle transport enquiries

What we believe

Clarity first

Verbal-only quotes hide extras. We push for clear details before you pay any advance.

Direct contact

WhatsApp and phone are primary. You always know who you are talking to.

Local focus

Tricity routes first — Zirakpur, Mohali, Chandigarh, Panchkula — done properly.

Honest note

Movers listed or matched through our desk are independent service providers. They are not our employees. Pricing depends on distance, inventory, date and materials. We do not invent awards, fake review counts or guaranteed lowest prices.
