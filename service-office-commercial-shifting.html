About office & commercial shifting

Office and commercial relocation needs different planning than a home move: access windows, lift booking, and equipment protection. Select Office as move size on the quote form and describe floors, lift availability and sensitive items.

Office moves done with less downtime

Commercial shifts work best with a written inventory of workstations, cabinets and IT assets. Many businesses prefer Friday evening or weekend slots so staff can resume on Monday.

Checklist for office relocation

  • Employee count / workstation count
  • Server racks, printers and specialised equipment
  • Building permissions for loading bay and lift
  • Labelling plan so boxes reach the right desk

FAQ — office shifting

Can you move only a few cabins? Yes — describe partial relocation in the notes field.

Do vendors dismantle furniture? Often yes, when requested; ask for it as a separate line item.
