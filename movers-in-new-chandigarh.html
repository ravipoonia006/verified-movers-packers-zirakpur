Local know-how

What's different about moving in New Chandigarh (Mullanpur)

New Chandigarh (Mullanpur) is GMADA's planned extension of the city, and most of what we see here are first-time move-ins to newly handed-over flats — which is a different job from a typical resale-flat move. Builder handover timelines, security clearance for first entry, and partially finished common areas all change what a mover needs to plan for.

Builder handover moves

If you're moving into a just-handed-over unit, mention that when you file your slip. Vendors familiar with New Chandigarh's newer societies know to check lift commissioning status and any builder-imposed move-in windows before turning up with a full crew.

Still-developing infrastructure

Some pockets of Mullanpur and the GMADA sectors are still finishing roads and common infrastructure, which can affect truck access more than in an established Chandigarh sector. Local vendors plan routes accordingly instead of discovering a blocked lane on move day.

A corridor town for now

Most residents here are still commuting into central Chandigarh, Mohali or Kharar for work, so a lot of move requests are actually two-way — people moving into New Chandigarh for larger, newer homes, and some moving out as they weigh commute time against space.

  • New-launch handover moves: confirm your builder's move-in slot before booking a vendor.
  • Under-construction society access: mention this upfront so the vendor plans extra time for security clearance.
  • Commute-driven moves: weekday moves are usually easier to schedule than weekends near the PGI Satellite Centre.
