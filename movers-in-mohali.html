Local know-how

What's different about moving in Mohali

Mohali's phase-numbering system trips up movers who don't work the city regularly — Phase 3B2 and Phase 3B1 sit right next to each other but are laid out very differently, and Phase 11 is nowhere near Phase 1 on the map. A lot of the "wrong address, wrong truck size" problems people run into with random movers come down to exactly this. We match you with vendors who already know the phase grid, not just the city name.

The Aerocity / IT City shift

A growing share of Mohali moves now involve the Aerocity and IT City corridor — corporate relocations, young professionals moving closer to their office, and companies shifting entire teams. These moves often need daytime or weekend slots to avoid disrupting a workday, and sometimes IT-equipment-specific packing (monitors, racks, sensitive electronics) that a purely residential mover won't quote correctly.

Older phases vs newer sectors

Phases 1 through 7 are older, established residential areas with independent kothis and low-rise flats — generally easier truck access. Sector 70, Balongi and the newer Landran Road belt have taller societies with stricter visitor and lift-booking rules, which changes how much lead time you should give a mover.

Best time to book

  • Corporate/office moves: book 5–7 days ahead so the vendor can plan around your building's loading-dock access hours.
  • Residential society moves: check your society's own move-in/move-out slot booking system before fixing a date with the mover.
  • End of month and start of month are the busiest — earlier requests get more vendor responses.
