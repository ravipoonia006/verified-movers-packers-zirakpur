Local know-how

What's different about moving in Gurgaon (Gurugram)

Gurgaon runs on corporate relocation timing more than most Indian cities — a huge share of moves here are tied to a joining date at a new company, which means the request often comes in with less lead time than a typical family move. Matching fast to a vendor who already works your sector matters more here than almost anywhere else.

High-rise societies with strict access rules

DLF and Golf Course Road societies commonly require lift bookings, security clearance for movers, and fixed move-in/move-out windows. Vendors familiar with these buildings plan around the rules instead of discovering them at the gate.

Cyber City office moves

Corporate relocations into or out of Cyber City and Udyog Vihar need daytime loading-dock slots and often IT-equipment-specific packing. Mention it's an office move when you file your slip so matched vendors quote accordingly.

A city still growing outward

Sohna Road and the sectors beyond 80 are newer and still finishing infrastructure in places — similar to New Chandigarh's situation, first-move-in societies need extra time for security clearance and lift commissioning.

  • Corporate relocations: book as early as your joining date allows — Gurgaon fills up fast on short notice.
  • DLF/Golf Course Road societies: confirm lift-booking slots with your society office before fixing a mover's time.
  • Newer Sohna Road societies: mention if it's a first move-in so vendors plan for security clearance.
