About specialty item handling

Specialty items need extra care, time and sometimes a larger crew. Listing them early avoids last-minute surprises. Use the notes field on the quote form so packing and handling can be planned properly.

Items that need special handling

Pianos, large mirrors, marble tops, aquariums and gym machines need more time and sometimes extra labour. Listing them early is the best way to avoid damage and surprise charges.

How to request

  • Name each specialty item in the form notes
  • Add approximate weight or size if you know it
  • Say whether stairs or a lift will be used

FAQ — specialty items

Do you move safes or industrial machines? Describe the item on WhatsApp; not every crew handles heavy industrial equipment.
